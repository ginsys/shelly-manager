Fixed
- An override or template that holds only empty, `false` or `0` plain values, such as `{"system":{"sntp":{"server":""}}}`, no longer leaves an empty section in the desired configuration that apply refuses. The route still answers 200 and stores the override as sent; only the desired configuration has no such section. A device that already holds such a section in its desired configuration is refused until its overrides are saved again or its templates change, which starts a recompute. Fixes ginsys/shelly-manager#843.

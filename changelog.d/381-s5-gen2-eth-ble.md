Changed
- A Gen2 or Gen3 Pro device now takes its Ethernet and Bluetooth RPC settings from a template: `network.eth` (`enable`, `ipv4mode`, static `ip`, `netmask`, `gw` and `nameserver`) is written with `Eth.SetConfig` and `system.device.ble.rpc` (`enable`, `keep_running`) with `BLE.SetConfig`, and both are read back. An Ethernet change is refused before anything is written when the manager reaches the device on its Ethernet address, or cannot prove it does not (it must have no Ethernet address, or use the Wi-Fi station address), when it comes with a Wi-Fi network change, when the device runs Ethernet in server mode, and when a static address has no address or no gateway. A device that does not report the `eth` or `ble` component is refused. Every Ethernet change owes a restart. The strand behaviour, the restart time and Bluetooth pairing are unconfirmed on hardware. Refs ginsys/shelly-manager#381.

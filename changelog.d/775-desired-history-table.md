Added
- Every real change to a device's desired configuration, by a template or override recompute or by an import, now leaves one history row with its old and new text, secrets masked; a device keeps its newest 50. Nothing shows or uses the rows yet, so the desired configuration of a device changes exactly as before. A recompute now writes only to the state it read, and reads again (up to three times) if the device changed meanwhile. Deleting a device deletes its rows. Refs ginsys/shelly-manager#775.
